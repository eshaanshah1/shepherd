10 · WHAT THIS LANGUAGE REFUSES
✕A status word next to a status mark. The mark is the state; the word is the tooltip.
✕Inverse video for selection. A fill plus a 2px edge, and the label stays legible.
✕Uppercase micro-labels with tracking. Sentence case at 11.5/600 says the same thing without the costume.
✕Repeating a name down the hierarchy. The task is named in the rail, the tab by its purpose, the pane by its tree.
✕A second hue for decoration. Sky, grass, wool, clay and red are spoken for; nothing else gets in.
✕Shadows for elevation. The luminance step is the elevation. One exception, §09, and it is written down.
✕Continuous animation. The meter is steps(1,end), so it repaints twice a second, not every frame.
✕Motion that moves a control. Colour changes over 140ms; positions do not change at all.
✕A row that grows for hover actions. They share one grid cell with the metadata.
✕Two primary buttons on one surface. If both are loud, neither is.
